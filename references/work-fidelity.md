# Work-text fidelity: RU / EN

Use these checks for work correspondence and any prose whose wording affects a reader's decision or an author's commitment. Protect meaning before improving style. These checks compare supplied text; they do not establish whether its claims are true or provide legal advice.

## Protected propositions

Before editing, identify the material propositions, including:

- Actor, recipient, object, amount, currency, dates, timezone, links, and requested action.
- Action status: proposal, plan, permission, obligation, approval, processing, completion.
- Certainty, attribution, frequency, approximation, quantifiers, negation, exceptions.
- Preconditions, eligibility, cancellation cutoff, sequence, and dependencies.
- Author position: disagreement, refusal, apology, thanks, concession, responsibility.
- Causal strength: contributing to a result is different from making it possible or guaranteeing it.
- Comparisons, rank, sequence, and simultaneity; evidence purpose and the basis of a disputed assessment.
- Offer framing and dependencies: a discretionary goodwill concession is different from accepting fault; an optional reduction is different from a completed refund.

Preserve their relationships. Keeping every number is insufficient when a condition, actor, status, or scope changes. Preserve intentionally unknown actors and implied boundaries rather than filling them in.

## Two-way comparison

1. Source to output: can the reader recover every material proposition and its qualification? Check that deadlines still govern the same action and exceptions the same rule.
2. Output to source: is every fact, commitment, reason, causal link, and author position supported? Check new first-person ownership, completed-action verbs, apologies, guarantees, and inferred eligibility.
3. Reader consequence: would the edit change what the reader expects, must do, may do, or understands the author to accept or dispute?

Fix any drift with the smallest correction. Keep an awkward but accurate phrase if needed. Do not rewrite from scratch as a default or add a causal narrative merely to join a list of facts. Do not remove a distinct claim because it is vague; only remove filler or genuinely duplicated meaning. In a requested summary, retain the conditions and qualifications attached to claims you include.

## Paired examples

The unsafe edits below change meaning; use them as checks rather than replacement templates.

| Source | Unsafe edit | Protected meaning |
|---|---|---|
| We're refunding £45. | We've refunded £45. | Processing does not prove completion. |
| Мы возвращаем вам 4 500 ₽. | Мы вернули вам 4 500 ₽. | Возврат в процессе не означает завершённый возврат. |
| Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. | Cancel before renewal to avoid the next charge. | The 24-hour cutoff governs cancellation. |
| Чтобы избежать следующего списания, отмените подписку не менее чем за 24 часа до продления. | Отмените подписку до продления. | Сохраняются срок и его связь со списанием. |
| We consider the deposit deduction justified. | We're sorry we wrongly deducted your deposit. | No apology or admission of error is supplied. |
| Считаем удержание из залога обоснованным. | Приносим извинения за ошибочное удержание. | Позиция автора остаётся прежней. |
| Tracking details will be sent after approval. | I'll send the tracking details today. | Actor is unspecified; approval is required; today is absent. |
| Данные для отслеживания отправят после согласования. | Я отправлю данные сегодня. | Исполнитель не назван; срок зависит от согласования. |
| This could help reduce delays. | This eliminates delays. | Possibility and partial reduction remain qualified. |
| Это может помочь сократить задержки. | Это исключит задержки. | Возможность не становится гарантией полного устранения. |
| Your visits helped us reach this milestone. | We couldn't have reached it without you. | Contribution does not establish necessity. |
| Ваши визиты помогли нам достичь этой цели. | Без вас мы бы не достигли этой цели. | Вклад не превращается в необходимую причину. |

Preserve source speech acts in both directions: do not add an apology where absent; do not erase `We're sorry about the delay` / `Приносим извинения за задержку` where present. Do not infer responsibility for a different issue from that apology. Likewise, `We can review your request` / `Мы можем рассмотреть ваш запрос` does not promise approval.

In a dispute, keep empathy (`I understand you're unhappy` / `Понимаем Ваше недовольство`) distinct from an apology for the contested act. Keep `as a goodwill gesture` / `в качестве жеста доброй воли`, the author's assessment of the evidence, and the customer's choice when present. Do not infer who caused damage from its existence or make a concession depend on quick settlement, agreement, or another unstated condition. Offering photos to explain the basis of a deduction must still explain that basis, rather than merely offer a view of the damage.

Preserve an unnamed actor even when their identity seems obvious. `The £150 deducted from your deposit` / `удержание 15 000 ₽ из залога` does not by itself support `we deducted` / `мы удержали`. Nearby `we consider the deduction justified` or `we can reduce it` supplies assessment or authority to change it, not the actor who performed it. Conversely, retain `we deducted` when the source explicitly says it. Do not convert an attributed assessment into an established fact or assign responsibility for the damage to the customer.

## Source priority and missing facts

Follow the user's stated source priority. If asked to write from notes, use those notes as the source and preserve the author's reasoning where useful. Treat a model-generated draft as a checklist of points to check, not as factual support. Do not discard a commitment from an approved text merely because rough notes omit it. Ask which source governs if a material conflict cannot be resolved from the request.

If accurate cleanup needs no additional facts, edit without questions. If a missing amount, deadline, actor, or decision prevents the requested result, ask only for that item. If the author skips an optional specificity question, retain the supported general statement or omit only a nonmaterial detail; never fabricate a number, anecdote, customer, or opinion.

## Limits of style checks

Treat punctuation, sentence-length variation, contractions, and word lists as editorial signals, not bans or evidence of authorship. Keep natural Russian dashes and meaningful English hedges. Do not optimize for detector scores, promise undetectability, or claim that a successful style scan proves semantic fidelity. Return the requested text without a compulsory audit, shorter-option footer, or interview unless needed to resolve a material ambiguity.
